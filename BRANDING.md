# 星笺 / Arcana Notes

「星」呼应星辰与指引；「笺」对应每日一牌与私人日记。英文名称 Arcana Notes 将塔罗与记录连接起来。名称未进行商标注册或可用性检索。

The Chinese name evokes a note from the stars. Arcana Notes connects tarot reflections with a personal journal. Trademark availability has not been assessed.
