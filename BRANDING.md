# 翠启 / Verdant Arcana

「翠」对应翡翠绿；「启」意味着启示与灵感。英文名称以 Verdant 呼应绿色，以 Arcana 呼应塔罗牌。名称用于本项目品牌展示，尚未进行商标注册或可用性检索。

The Chinese name combines emerald and insight. The English name connects a verdant palette with tarot's arcana. Trademark availability has not been assessed.
